# Product Hunt launch checklist

## Two weeks before
- [ ] Confirm product is usable by strangers (no broken links, clear onboarding)
- [ ] Write maker comment draft (story + who it's for + one ask)
- [ ] Prepare gallery: logo, 3–5 screenshots or short GIF, optional demo video
- [ ] Draft tagline (≤60 chars) and description
- [ ] Build a notification list (email/Twitter/Discord) — no spam
- [ ] Decide hunter vs self-hunt

## One week before
- [ ] Create Product Hunt draft; upload assets
- [ ] Ask 5–10 friendly makers to be available launch morning (not for fake upvotes — for honest feedback)
- [ ] Schedule social posts + email for launch day
- [ ] Prep FAQ answers (pricing, platforms, roadmap)

## Launch day
- [ ] Publish at ~12:01am PT (or your planned slot)
- [ ] Post maker first comment within the first hour
- [ ] Reply to every comment the same day
- [ ] Share launch link with your list once (clear CTA)
- [ ] Monitor ranking; don't beg for upvotes in unrelated communities

## Day after
- [ ] Thank supporters publicly
- [ ] Note top objections from comments → product/FAQ updates
- [ ] Capture screenshots of the launch page for social proof
- [ ] Write a short retrospective (what worked / what to change next launch)
