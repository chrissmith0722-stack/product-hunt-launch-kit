# Tagline formulas (≤60 characters)

Fill the brackets; keep language concrete.

1. **Outcome for audience**  
   `[Outcome] for [audience]`  
   Example: `Launch checklists for indie SaaS founders`

2. **Replace the pain**  
   `[Do X] without [pain]`  
   Example: `Ship waitlists without a backend`

3. **Tool + job**  
   `The [simple tool] to [job-to-be-done]`  
   Example: `The simple CLI to mint license keys`

4. **Before → after**  
   `From [messy state] to [clear state]`  
   Example: `From messy folders to clean download ZIPs`

5. **Numbered specificity**  
   `[N] [things] to [outcome]`  
   Example: `12 templates to launch your digital product`

## Tips
- Prefer verbs and nouns buyers already search for.
- Avoid buzzwords: revolutionary, AI-powered, seamless.
- Say the product category out loud — if a stranger can't repeat it, shorten it.
