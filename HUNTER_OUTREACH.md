# Hunter outreach template

Use when asking someone to hunt (or co-hunt) your launch. Keep it short and easy to decline.

---

Subject: Product Hunt hunt request for {{product_name}}?

Hi {{hunter_first_name}},

I'm launching **{{product_name}}** on Product Hunt on **{{launch_date}}** — it's {{one_sentence_what_it_does}}.

Would you be open to hunting it? I've attached:
- Draft tagline + description
- Gallery assets / demo link: {{demo_url}}
- Maker comment draft

Happy to self-hunt if timing doesn't work — either way, no pressure.

Thanks for considering it,
{{your_name}}
{{your_profile_url}}

---

## Self-hunt notes
- Self-hunting is fine; focus energy on product quality and maker replies.
- Don't offer payment for upvotes or rankings.
- Do offer a clear story and a working product.
